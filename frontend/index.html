Tournament Information & Rules

1. Schedule & Venue

The SPL 2026 Season 5 will take place from October 15 to October 18, 2026 at the state-of-the-art SPL Cricket Arena. Matches will start daily at 8:00 AM under ICC-standard T20 guidelines with professional white leather balls.

2. Registration Guidelines

Registration fee is ₹500 per player. This includes the entry fee, match refreshments, and a custom tournament dry-fit T-shirt branded with your preferred name and number. Payments are processed securely via Razorpay UPI, cards, and Net Banking.

3. Squad and Kit Selection

All registered players will be placed in the auction pool. Playing role and T-shirt size options cannot be altered after registration is completed, so please double-check your T-shirt size (S, M, L, XL, XXL) and role.

4. Code of Conduct

All players must adhere to strictly fair play regulations. Standard ICC rules govern all matches, with qualified, independent local umpires. Decision of the tournament committee remains final and binding.
